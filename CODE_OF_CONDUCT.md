# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As members of this project team and contributors, we pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

We are committed to creating an environment where everyone feels comfortable sharing ideas, asking questions, providing feedback, and learning from one another.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and inclusive.
- Listening to different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving constructive feedback and accepting feedback professionally.
- Communicating clearly and respectfully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Supporting equal participation among team members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or threatening language.
- Disrespectful comments about someone's background, identity, or abilities.
- Deliberately disrupting team discussions or collaboration.
- Sharing private or confidential information without permission.
- Any other behaviour that creates an intimidating or hostile environment.

## Responsibilities and Consequences

All project members and contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

Behaviour that violates these standards may result in a warning, removal from a discussion or project activity, or escalation to the course instructor or appropriate university support, depending on the seriousness of the incident.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates these standards, please contact the project team through a private communication channel or raise the concern with the course instructor.

All concerns will be treated seriously and handled respectfully. The team will make reasonable efforts to maintain confidentiality and avoid retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
